# GFM extended autolinks

Examples follow https://github.github.io/gfm/#autolinks-extension-.
The md4c permissive-autolink corpus uses different punctuation and scheme rules.

```````````````````````````````` example
https://example.com
www.example.com
person@example.com
.
<p><a href="https://example.com">https://example.com</a>
<a href="http://www.example.com">www.example.com</a>
<a href="mailto:person@example.com">person@example.com</a></p>
.
--fautolinks
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
Visit www.commonmark.org/help for more information.
.
<p>Visit <a href="http://www.commonmark.org/help">www.commonmark.org/help</a> for more information.</p>
.
--fautolinks
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
www.google.com/search?q=Markup+(business)))

(www.google.com/search?q=Markup+(business))

www.google.com/search?q=(business))+ok
.
<p><a href="http://www.google.com/search?q=Markup+(business)">www.google.com/search?q=Markup+(business)</a>))</p>
<p>(<a href="http://www.google.com/search?q=Markup+(business)">www.google.com/search?q=Markup+(business)</a>)</p>
<p><a href="http://www.google.com/search?q=(business))+ok">www.google.com/search?q=(business))+ok</a></p>
.
--fautolinks
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
www.google.com/search?q=commonmark&hl=en

www.google.com/search?q=commonmark&hl;

www.commonmark.org/he<lp
.
<p><a href="http://www.google.com/search?q=commonmark&amp;hl=en">www.google.com/search?q=commonmark&amp;hl=en</a></p>
<p><a href="http://www.google.com/search?q=commonmark">www.google.com/search?q=commonmark</a>&amp;hl;</p>
<p><a href="http://www.commonmark.org/he">www.commonmark.org/he</a>&lt;lp</p>
.
--fautolinks
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
a.b-c_d@a.b

a.b-c_d@a.b.

a.b-c_d@a.b-

a.b-c_d@a.b_

hello@mail+xyz.example isn't valid, but hello+xyz@mail.example is.
.
<p><a href="mailto:a.b-c_d@a.b">a.b-c_d@a.b</a></p>
<p><a href="mailto:a.b-c_d@a.b">a.b-c_d@a.b</a>.</p>
<p>a.b-c_d@a.b-</p>
<p>a.b-c_d@a.b_</p>
<p>hello@mail+xyz.example isn't valid, but <a href="mailto:hello+xyz@mail.example">hello+xyz@mail.example</a> is.</p>
.
--fautolinks
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
mailto:a.b-c_d@a.b/

xmpp:foo@bar.baz/txt@bin.com

xmpp:foo@bar.baz/txt/bin
.
<p><a href="mailto:a.b-c_d@a.b">mailto:a.b-c_d@a.b</a>/</p>
<p><a href="xmpp:foo@bar.baz/txt@bin.com">xmpp:foo@bar.baz/txt@bin.com</a></p>
<p><a href="xmpp:foo@bar.baz/txt">xmpp:foo@bar.baz/txt</a>/bin</p>
.
--fautolinks
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
**https://example.com** and ~~www.example.com~~.

[www.example.com](https://target.example) and `www.example.com`.

[www.example.com][target]

[target]: https://target.example
.
<p><strong><a href="https://example.com">https://example.com</a></strong> and <del><a href="http://www.example.com">www.example.com</a></del>.</p>
<p><a href="https://target.example">www.example.com</a> and <code>www.example.com</code>.</p>
<p><a href="https://target.example">www.example.com</a></p>
.
--fautolinks
--fstrikethrough
````````````````````````````````
