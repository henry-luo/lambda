Bridges and Ferries of the River Town

The river town woke slowly, with fishermen untangling nets, bakers stacking warm loaves, and children racing along the muddy banks before the school bell rang across the square.

Local historians, who have studied the region for decades, say the harbor once handled more trade than any port within a hundred miles, shipping timber, wool, and salted fish.

Today the old warehouses hold galleries, workshops, and small cafés, while a restored ferry carries visitors between the two halves of the town every hour during summer.
