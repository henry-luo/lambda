\section{Orthogonal components}
A movement of four centimetres horizontally and three centimetres vertically
has a five-centimetre resultant. The diagram shows the two components and
the straight-line displacement between the start and end points.

\begin{figure}
\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \draw[blue] (0,0) -- (4cm,0) -- (4cm,30mm);
  \draw[red] (0,0) -- (4cm,30mm);
  \draw[gray] (4cm,0) -- (4cm,30mm);
  \node at (2cm,-0.3cm) {$x=4$};
  \node at (4.4cm,1.5cm) {$y=3$};
  \node at (1.7cm,2cm) {$r=5$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\caption{Displacement resolved into horizontal and vertical components.}
\label{fig:displacement}
\end{figure}

The unit-bearing coordinates also check that millimetres and centimetres
share the same drawing basis.
