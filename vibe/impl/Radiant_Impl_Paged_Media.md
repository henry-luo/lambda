# Radiant multiple views and paged-media implementation

**Status:** in progress, 2026-10-07. The complete publishing proposal remains the delivery scope; completion of the view model does not complete pagination.
**Design authority:** [Radiant_Layout_Paged_Media](../radiant/Radiant_Layout_Paged_Media.md), including multiple views, preview roots, page selection and book spreads.
**Formal linkage:** D4.5.1v4 (Radiant regions/pools and generation handles), D4.1.4v5 (allocator lifetime and single-writer publication), D5.3.3/D5.4.1 (precise callback/evaluator ownership), D7.1.2v2/D7.1.4v2 (resource layering and headless profiles), D7.3.6 (test-host export allowlist). Geometry follows RSC1 and RSC6–RSC8 in [Radiant_Scale](../radiant/Radiant_Scale.md). No formal language ruling is changed.

## Delivery sequence

| P5: publishing composition | Footnote reservation/splitting, deferred floats/flushes, running content, counters, targets, TOC/leaders and convergence | Joint body/note/float/reference fixtures, rollback and diagnosed nonconvergence; non-DOM glue/penalty/policy fixtures |
| P6: output and entry points | Fragment-aware paint input, physical PDF page loop, glyph-position preservation, preview raster paint, export selection, opt-in options and A4 | Audit probe becomes three 240×360-point pages; PDF structure/raster checks; preview placement/filtering independent of export |

P1 supplies geometry/presentation mechanisms for P6. P2–P4 now compose actual secondary block/inline views; P4–P6 remain partial. The CLI must not advertise complete publishing support before the remaining producer, continuation and composition gates pass. The real TeX/LaTeX producer is future work; extensibility contracts and their tests belong to this delivery.
